Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Zero Server-Side Data Collection

StreamPulse operates as a purely static website. We do not maintain any user database, server-side user tracking logs, or private accounts on our servers.

2. Client-Side LocalStorage

We utilize standard browser localStorage exclusively to preserve your chosen interface language preference (e.g., English or Indonesian). No personally identifiable information (PII) is recorded or transmitted.

3. Third-Party Advertising & Adsterra

This website may display advertisements served by third-party ad networks, including Adsterra. These providers may use standard web cookies, web beacons, or device identifiers in accordance with their respective privacy policies to deliver relevant advertising. You may manage cookie preferences via your web browser settings.

4. External Video Providers

Embedded video players (such as YouTube or Videy) are governed by the respective terms and privacy policies of those third-party services.
